# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of the rippled-portable-builds project pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community focused on the secure distribution of rippled binaries.

## Our Standards

### Positive Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* **Security First**: Prioritizing security in all contributions and discussions
* **Transparency**: Being open about processes, changes, and decision-making
* **Accuracy**: Providing accurate information about binaries, signatures, and procedures
* **Respect**: Showing empathy and kindness toward other community members
* **Constructive Feedback**: Giving and gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* **Accountability**: Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes
* **Community Focus**: Prioritizing what is best for the overall community and XRPL network
* **Professional Conduct**: Maintaining professional behavior in all interactions

### Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

* **Security Violations**:
  - Distributing unsigned or improperly signed binaries
  - Bypassing security verification processes
  - Sharing private keys or sensitive credentials
  - Deliberately introducing malicious code or backdoors

* **Governance Violations**:
  - Making unauthorized changes to release binaries
  - Misrepresenting binary provenance or authenticity
  - Circumventing review processes
  - Abusing maintainer privileges

* **Community Violations**:
  - Harassment, trolling, or insulting/derogatory comments
  - Public or private harassment
  - Publishing others' private information without permission
  - Sexual attention or advances of any kind
  - Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate

## Governance Structure

### Roles and Responsibilities

1. **Maintainers**
   - Review and approve pull requests
   - Verify binary signatures and integrity
   - Manage repository access
   - Respond to security reports
   - Enforce this Code of Conduct

2. **Contributors**
   - Follow security guidelines
   - Provide accurate documentation
   - Participate constructively in discussions
   - Report security issues responsibly

3. **Users**
   - Verify binaries before use
   - Report issues through proper channels
   - Provide constructive feedback
   - Follow security best practices

### Decision-Making Process

1. **Regular Changes**
   - Pull requests require maintainer review
   - Documentation changes reviewed for accuracy
   - Configuration updates verified for security

2. **Security-Critical Changes**
   - Multiple maintainer approval required
   - Security review mandatory
   - Verification of GPG signatures
   - Testing in non-production environment

3. **Binary Additions**
   - Must come from official XRPLF sources
   - GPG signature verification required
   - Checksum verification when available
   - Documentation update required
   - Announcement to community

### Conflict Resolution

1. **Minor Disagreements**: Resolved through discussion in issues/PRs
2. **Technical Disputes**: Escalated to maintainer consensus
3. **Security Concerns**: Immediate escalation to security team
4. **Code of Conduct Violations**: Handled per enforcement guidelines below

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to:
- Remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions not aligned with this Code of Conduct
- Ban temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful
- Report security violations to appropriate authorities

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including:
- GitHub repository (issues, PRs, discussions)
- Documentation
- Communication channels
- Events or presentations representing the project

It also applies when representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers will follow these Community Impact Guidelines in determining consequences:

### 1. Correction
**Community Impact**: Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior.

**Consequence**: Private written warning, providing clarity around the nature of the violation and an explanation of why the behavior was inappropriate. A public apology may be requested.

### 2. Warning
**Community Impact**: A violation through a single incident or series of actions.

**Consequence**: A warning with consequences for continued behavior. No interaction with involved parties for a specified period. This includes avoiding interactions in community spaces and external channels. Violating these terms may lead to a temporary or permanent ban.

### 3. Temporary Ban
**Community Impact**: A serious violation of community standards, including sustained inappropriate behavior.

**Consequence**: Temporary ban from any sort of interaction or public communication with the community for a specified period. No public or private interaction with involved parties is allowed during this period. Violating these terms may lead to a permanent ban.

### 4. Permanent Ban
**Community Impact**: Demonstrating a pattern of violation, sustained inappropriate behavior, harassment, or aggression toward individuals or groups.

**Consequence**: Permanent ban from any sort of public interaction within the community.

### Security Violations

**Community Impact**: 
- Distributing compromised or malicious binaries
- Bypassing signature verification
- Sharing sensitive credentials
- Other severe security breaches

**Consequence**: 
- Immediate permanent ban
- Removal of all contributions if necessary
- Report to relevant authorities if applicable
- Public disclosure if required for community safety

## Transparency and Accountability

### Public Record

- All enforcement actions (except security-sensitive) are documented
- Maintainer decisions are explained when requested
- Community members can appeal decisions through established channels

### Regular Review

This Code of Conduct is reviewed:
- Quarterly for effectiveness
- After any major incident
- When community feedback suggests changes needed

### Amendments

Changes to this Code of Conduct require:
- Public proposal with rationale
- Community feedback period (minimum 14 days)
- Maintainer consensus
- Clear changelog

## Our Commitment

We are committed to:
- Maintaining the highest security standards
- Transparent governance
- Fair and consistent enforcement
- Continuous improvement
- Community safety and well-being
- The integrity of the XRPL network
